# Prompt: Thumbnail Generation — Midnight Telugu

## Purpose

Create a compelling thumbnail that gets clicks without being misleading or sensational.

## Thumbnail Principles

### Visual Composition
- One dominant visual element (face or key scene object)
- High contrast — must be readable at small size
- Dark or atmospheric background
- Slight vignette for focus

### Text (Optional, Minimal)
- At most 3–4 Telugu words
- Large, bold, readable font
- Positioned at bottom or top — not center
- No all-caps screaming text

### Emotional Signal
- Curiosity over shock
- Question in the viewer's mind, not explicit spoiler
- Tense, not horrifying
- No fake tears, fake fear, or clickbait expressions

### What NOT to Do
- No misleading images (don't show something that's not in the video)
- No graphic violence
- No sexual content
- No political imagery
- No celebrity faces or impersonation
- No exaggerated facial expressions typical of clickbait thumbnails
- No fake breaking-news style

## Telugu Thumbnail Text Examples (Good)
- "ఆ తలుపు వెనక..."
- "అర్థరాత్రి రహస్యం"
- "నమ్మలేరు"
- "చివరికి నిజం..."

## Thumbnail Prompt Format

```
"Cinematic vertical thumbnail, [key visual], dark atmospheric background,
[mood] lighting, minimal Telugu text '[text]' in bold at bottom,
no celebrities, no gore, photorealistic, high contrast, 1080x1920"
```
