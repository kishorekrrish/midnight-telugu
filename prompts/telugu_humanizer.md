# Prompt: Telugu Humanizer — Midnight Telugu

## Task

Take a Telugu script and make it sound like a natural human storyteller — not AI, not textbook, not news-reader.

## What to Fix

### Lexical Substitutions
Replace formal/textbook words with natural spoken equivalents:
- "చెప్పాడు" → "అన్నాడు" (when natural)
- "ఆశ్చర్యపడ్డాడు" → "నోరు తెరుచుకుపోయింది"
- "వాస్తవం" → "నిజం"
- "ప్రజలు" → "మనుషులు"
- "అందువల్ల" → "అందుకే"
- "పరిస్థితి" → "సంగతి"

### Rhythm and Pacing
- Break very long sentences into two shorter ones
- Add em-dash pauses (—) before reveals
- Vary sentence length — short for punch, longer for description
- Add breathing space between paragraphs

### Tone Calibration
- Preserve emotional weight — do not flatten the drama
- Do not add new plot elements — only improve language
- Keep the story meaning exactly the same
- Remove any preachy or moralistic phrasing

### Natural Closing
Ensure the final line invites audience reaction naturally:
- "మీకు ఇలాంటి అనుభవం ఉందా?"
- "ఇది నిజంగా జరిగింది అని మీరు నమ్ముతారా?"
- "Comment లో చెప్పండి"

## What NOT to Change

- The plot
- The twist
- The key emotional moments
- Telugu vocabulary that is already natural

## Language Boundaries

- Neutral Andhra + Telangana mix
- Not heavy Hyderabadi slang
- Not formal textbook Telugu
- Not Anglicized
