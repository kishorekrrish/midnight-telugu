# Monetization Safety — Midnight Telugu

## YouTube Monetization Requirements

To maintain monetization eligibility, every video must pass these checks:

### Originality
- ✅ Original stories only — no copied Reddit posts, news stories, or film plots
- ✅ All characters are fictional
- ✅ No real-person impersonation
- ✅ No celebrity references

### Content Safety
- ✅ No graphic violence
- ✅ No sexual content
- ✅ No hate speech
- ✅ No political controversy
- ✅ No religious controversy
- ✅ No medical/legal/financial misinformation
- ✅ No misleading thumbnails
- ✅ No clickbait that contradicts content

### Reused Content Risk
YouTube can demonetize channels that heavily reuse content from other creators. This system generates **original** stories using templates, not scraped content. Always verify:
- The story premise has not appeared in other Telugu channels
- The hook line is unique
- The visual style is original

### Why No Auto-Upload in v1

Auto-upload creates risk:
1. AI-generated content may fail human review
2. A bad video hurts the entire channel's reputation
3. YouTube's spam policies target automated upload pipelines
4. No monetization review step means monetizable content gets mixed with non-monetizable

**v1 policy: Every video is reviewed by Kishore before upload.**
