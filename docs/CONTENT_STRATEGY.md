# Content Strategy — Midnight Telugu

## Channel Positioning

**Midnight Telugu** delivers cinematic Telugu Shorts with suspense, mystery, emotion, and unexpected endings.

Target: Telugu-speaking audiences (Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, diaspora) aged 18–40.

## Content Buckets

| # | Bucket | Description |
|---|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Midnight Mystery | Strange occurrences after dark, unexplained events |
| 2 | Village Mystery | Rural secrets, folk-horror, generational mysteries |
| 3 | Family Suspense | Hidden truths within families, emotional reveals |
| 4 | Psychological Twist | Mind-bending reveals, unreliable narrator |
| 5 | Strange Event | Real-feeling supernatural or bizarre occurrences |
| 6 | Soft Horror | Atmospheric, not graphic — dread, not gore |
| 7 | Crime (No Violence) | Clever crime, investigation, justice — no graphic scenes |
| 8 | Emotional Suspense | Grief, love, sacrifice with a suspenseful reveal |
| 9 | Karma / Justice | Wrongdoers meeting their comeuppance through story |
| 10 | Poor vs Rich | Class struggle with a twist ending |

## Story Format (YouTube Shorts)

- **Total Duration:** 45–60 seconds
- **Structure:**
  1. Hook (0–3s): Immediately gripping — one sentence that creates a question
  2. Setup (3–15s): Who, where, what's wrong
  3. Build-up (15–40s): Tension escalation, stakes raised
  4. Twist/Reveal (40–52s): Unexpected turn
  5. Closing Line (52–60s): Emotional landing or open question

## Quality Bar

- Every story must feel like a real person telling it, not a content farm.
- No repetitive AI patterns (e.g., "in a shocking turn of events").
- No cheap moral-story spam.
- No celebrity references or real-person impersonation.
- No copyrighted characters or film plots.
- No political or religious controversy.
- Family-safe and monetization-safe.
