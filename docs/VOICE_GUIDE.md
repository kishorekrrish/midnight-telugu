# Voice Guide — Midnight Telugu

## Target Voice Character

A calm but intense storyteller. Think of someone who has seen things they can't explain — they're not screaming, but every word carries weight.

## Tone Spectrum

```
Calm ←————————————————————→ Urgent
     [start]         [build] [reveal]
```

- **Opening:** Conversational, slightly hushed. Pull the listener in.
- **Build-up:** Gradually more tense. Pace slows at key details.
- **Reveal:** Short pause before. Then direct delivery.
- **Ending:** Calm again — reflective, not dramatic.

## Pacing Guidelines

- **Words per minute:** 120–140 (slower than normal conversation)
- **Pause at em-dashes:** 0.5–1 second
- **Pause at paragraph breaks:** 0.8–1.5 seconds
- **Emphasis:** Place on the unexpected or emotionally loaded word

## Pronunciation Notes

- Telugu words should be pronounced as native Telugu, not Hinglish-influenced
- English loanwords (phone, camera, office) pronounced naturally in context
- Place names: natural local pronunciation

## What to Avoid

- Screeching or fake horror voice
- Over-dramatic crying during narration
- Robotic flat delivery
- Speed-reading to fit time
- Breathlessness throughout (save it for climax)
